MediVoice Dental Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions

GENERAL
Q: Where is the clinic located?
A: MediVoice Dental Clinic is located in Kerala, India. Please call us for the exact address and directions.

Q: What are your working hours?
A: We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Q: Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?
A: We recommend booking an appointment to avoid waiting. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to doctor availability.

Q: How do I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
A: You can cancel or reschedule by calling this number. Please give at least 2 hours notice for cancellations.

PAYMENTS
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit Card, Debit Card, and Net Banking.

Q: Is EMI available?
A: Yes, EMI is available for treatments above ₹10,000. Ask our staff for details.

Q: Do you accept insurance?
A: We accept most major dental insurance plans. Please bring your insurance card when you visit.

TREATMENTS
Q: Is root canal treatment painful?
A: Root canal treatment is done under local anaesthesia so you will not feel pain during the procedure. There may be mild soreness for 1-2 days after.

Q: How long does teeth whitening last?
A: Professional teeth whitening results last between 6 months to 2 years depending on your diet and oral hygiene habits.

Q: How long does braces treatment take?
A: Braces treatment typically takes 12 to 24 months depending on the complexity of your case. Our doctors will give you a personalised estimate during consultation.

Q: At what age can my child get braces?
A: Braces are generally recommended after most permanent teeth have come in, usually between ages 11 and 14. We offer free consultation for children.

EMERGENCY
Q: Do you handle dental emergencies?
A: Yes, we handle dental emergencies during working hours. For severe tooth pain, broken teeth, or swelling, call us immediately and we will fit you in as soon as possible.
